Research Project

Financial Performance & Profitability Analysis

Nestlé India vs Britannia Industries

A five-year comparative income statement analysis of two major companies in India's food and FMCG market.

Project Overview

Using consolidated income statement data expressed in ₹ crore, the study follows sales, expenses, operating profit, profit before tax and net profit to understand cost structure and profitability.

Basis of comparison: consolidated income statement figures for comparable 12-month periods. Nestlé India’s 15-month financial-year transition remains a limitation.
