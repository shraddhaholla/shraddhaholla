Research Project

Risk Interconnection in Business Organisations

Boeing 737 MAX · Nestlé India Maggi

When one business risk triggers another: a comparative study of operational, safety, regulatory, reputational and financial risks.

Project Overview

The project examines interconnected business risks through two case studies from different industries: Boeing’s 737 MAX crisis and Nestlé India’s Maggi controversy.

Financial figures are company-wide where stated. Nestlé India’s sales and profit decline is not attributed entirely to Maggi.
