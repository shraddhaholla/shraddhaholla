Research Project

Risk Interconnection in Business Organisations

Boeing 737 MAX · Nestlé India Maggi

When one business risk triggers another: a comparative study of operational, safety, regulatory, reputational and financial risks.

Project Overview

The project examines interconnected business risks through two case studies from different industries: Boeing’s 737 MAX crisis and Nestlé India’s Maggi controversy.

Financial figures are company-wide where stated. Nestlé India’s sales and profit decline is not attributed entirely to Maggi.

Project Contents

Understanding Risk Interconnection

Risks influence one another rather than occurring in isolation.

Risk interconnection refers to the way different risks influence one another within a business organisation. A problem that begins in one area can create further difficulties in operations, regulatory compliance, reputation and financial performance.

01Initial product or safety concern
02Regulatory and operational impact
03Reputational and financial impact
This is a conceptual network, not a fixed sequence. Risks can influence one another in multiple directions and may occur concurrently.
