Research Project

Sunk Costs vs Opportunity Costs

Amazon · Ford · Tata Motors

Should money already spent influence what a company invests in next? What does a company sacrifice when it chooses one investment over another?

Project Overview

The study applies relevant-cost concepts to three real company cases and examines how past expenditure and alternative uses of resources matter in investment decisions.

Historical estimates are labelled as such. Opportunity costs are not monetarily quantified where public evidence does not establish a value.
